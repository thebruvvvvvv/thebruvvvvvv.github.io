Frederick

I grew up in a small suburban town. Since my youth, I've been obsessed with etymology,
collecting all sorts of small critters, such as centipedes and pill bugs.
(Neither of which are actually insects.)

My mother and I frequently went hiking ever since i was in my preteens, which is probaly the main reason that I got into etymology.
Though, these interests made me somewhat of an outcast from my peers, as the others often see the critters I adore as mere vermin.
This would all change when I got into middle school and gained access to the interwebs and found communities composed of like-minded individuals.
